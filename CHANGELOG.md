## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-14

First playable version of the web app.

### Added

- **Capture**: take or upload a photo of a log page, or try the included sample page
- **Detect**: straightens a tilted photo and finds the review boxes from the printed lines; corners can be dragged
- **Ink only**: removes the paper so only the handwriting and printed lines remain, on a transparent background
- **Layouts**: four 1080 × 1920 Story layouts that size themselves to the review and keep clear of Instagram's top and bottom bars
- **Card styles**: Clear, Paper, Gray, Space, Film and Ticket, with any text colour or the real pen colour
- **Photos**: add photos to each slot, then move and zoom them (drag, pinch, scroll)
- **Share and keep**: PNG export, share sheet on phones, and a "send to My Log" animation
- **My Log**: book view with an editable cover, flip by swipe, arrows or keys, two-page spread on wide screens, and a grid view
- **Your data**: storage usage, `.movielog` backup export and import, save every page to a folder (Chrome / Edge on desktop), delete all
- **Brand**: hand-drawn ticket logo, favicon and iPhone icon; gray page with Inter Tight headlines, DM Mono details and a `MENU / PAGE` header with a full-screen menu
